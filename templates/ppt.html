AI Smart Study Planner

SLIDE 02

📌 Introduction

Students often face difficulty managing subjects, exam dates, marks and study time.

The AI Smart Study Planner provides an intelligent solution that analyzes academic information and generates a personalized study schedule.

SLIDE 03

❗ Problem Statement

  • Difficulty in managing multiple subjects.
  • Lack of personalized study schedules.
  • Difficulty identifying high-priority subjects.
  • Manual planning consumes time.
  • Students may not allocate enough time to difficult subjects.
SLIDE 04

🎯 Objectives

  • Analyze student academic performance.
  • Calculate subject priority.
  • Consider upcoming examination dates.
  • Generate personalized study plans.
  • Track student study progress.
  • Provide a simple web-based interface.

SLIDE 08

🧠 AI Priority Calculation

The system calculates a priority score for each subject using important academic factors.

  • Student marks / performance
  • Upcoming examination date
  • Subject difficulty
  • Remaining topics
Result Subjects receive a priority level such as Low, Medium or High.
SLIDE 09

📅 Study Plan Generation

The study planner uses subject priority scores to distribute available study sessions.

  • Identifies subject priorities.
  • Calculates remaining topics.
  • Distributes study sessions.
  • Creates a seven-day study schedule.
  • Assigns study dates and time slots.
