AI Smart Study Planner

1. Abstract

The AI Smart Study Planner is a web-based application designed to help students organize their studies according to their academic performance, subject difficulty, completed topics and upcoming examinations.

The system uses AI-based priority calculation to identify subjects that require greater attention. Based on the calculated priority, the system generates a personalized study plan for the student.

The application also provides subject management, examination management, progress tracking and study-session management through an interactive web interface.

2. Introduction

Students often have multiple subjects, different levels of difficulty and examinations scheduled on different dates. Managing all these factors manually can make it difficult to decide which subject should be studied first.

The AI Smart Study Planner solves this problem by analyzing student information and generating a priority score for each subject. The system then uses these priorities to create a structured study plan.

3. Problem Statement

Students may struggle to create an effective study schedule because of multiple subjects, different subject difficulties, varying academic performance and approaching examinations.

A system is therefore required to analyze these factors and generate a personalized study plan that helps students focus on important subjects.

4. Objectives

  • To develop a web-based smart study planning system.
  • To store student and academic information using MySQL.
  • To calculate subject priority using AI-based logic.
  • To consider examination dates while planning study sessions.
  • To generate personalized study plans.
  • To track completed topics and study progress.
  • To provide students with a simple and interactive interface.

5. Existing System

In a traditional study planning approach, students generally prepare their study schedules manually. The schedule may not automatically consider subject difficulty, examination dates, marks and remaining topics.

Limitations

  • Manual study-plan preparation.
  • Difficult to manage multiple subjects.
  • No automatic priority calculation.
  • Limited progress tracking.
  • Study plans may not adapt to academic information.

6. Proposed System

The proposed AI Smart Study Planner provides an automated approach to study planning. The system collects subject, examination and progress information and calculates a priority score for each subject.

The generated priority values are then used by the study-plan generator to distribute study sessions according to subject importance.

10. AI Priority Calculation

The AI component calculates a priority score for every subject. The priority calculation considers factors such as examination urgency, student performance, subject difficulty and remaining topics.

Priority Factors

Examination urgency + Performance + Difficulty + Remaining topics

Subjects with higher priority receive greater importance during study-plan generation.

11. Study Plan Generation

The study planner receives the subjects and their calculated priority scores. Higher-priority subjects are assigned more study sessions.

The planner generates study sessions for a seven-day period according to the available study hours.

Study Plan Process

  • Collect student subjects.
  • Calculate subject priority.
  • Identify remaining topics.
  • Distribute study sessions.
  • Assign study dates and times.
  • Generate topics for each session.
  • Store the generated plan in MySQL.

12. Database Design

MySQL is used to store the application's student and academic information.

Main Tables

  • students — stores student account details.
  • subjects — stores subject marks, difficulty and topic progress.
  • exams — stores examination dates.
  • study_plans — stores generated study sessions.
  • progress — stores study and learning progress.
